Free beta for macOS and Windows

The real app checking an email with a local model (Qwen 3.6 in LM Studio). Hover a fix to see where it is.

Fixes only what's wrong

Grammar mistakes and anything a reader could misread. If a sentence is understandable and correct, it stays the way you wrote it.

Knows where you're writing

Pick Slack, GitHub, a formal email or your own setting. "thx" is fine in Slack. In an email to a client, it gets fixed.

Keeps suggestions separate

Now and then it offers a more natural way to say something. Suggestions sit in their own column and never count as mistakes.

Uses the model you choose

An OpenRouter or OpenAI key, or a model on your own computer through LM Studio or Ollama. Your history stays on your computer.

Notices what keeps coming back

A mistake that shows up in different texts on different days goes on a short watch list, with a quick "fix your own sentence" review. One-off typos don't count.

Progress counts mistakes per 100 words and only calls something a trend when the change is bigger than chance.
