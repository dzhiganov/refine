Fewer mistakes in your English and German.

Paste what you wrote. Refine fixes the mistakes, explains each one in plain words, and leaves the rest of your text alone.

Download

Free beta · use at your own risk

Changes only what it explains

Every change is a listed mistake with a short reason: grammar, spelling, wrong words, or anything a reader could misread. Correct sentences stay the way you wrote them. Ideas for more natural phrasing sit separately and never count as mistakes.

Tell it where the text is going

Pick Slack, GitHub, a formal email, or make your own. "thx" is fine in Slack. In an email to a client, it gets fixed.

Notices what keeps coming back

Make the same mistake in another text on another day, and it goes on a short watch list (up to three) with a quick review: fix your own old sentence. A one-off slip doesn't.

Download

Latest version. The app is free.

To check texts, Refine needs an AI model: an account with OpenRouter or OpenAI (you paste in a key from their site, and they charge for use), or a free model on your own computer through LM Studio or Ollama. Your texts go only to the model you choose. Your history stays on your computer.

Beta: use at your own risk

Refine is beta software. It may not work, may crash, and could harm your computer or lose your data. It comes as is, with no warranty. You use it at your own risk, and the people who make Refine aren't responsible for any damage or loss. Corrections come from an AI model and can be wrong, so check anything important before you send it.

Refine isn't registered with Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer will warn you the first time.

Mac: open the .dmg, drag Refine into Applications, and open it. When macOS says it can't check the app, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows: if you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Prefer an .msi? Download it here.
